## MCQ 1
Question: What is the primary motivation for developing this decentralized file-sharing platform?
A) To explore cutting-edge technologies like blockchain and IPFS.
B) To enhance security and privacy in digital content distribution.
C) To create a user-friendly interface for file management.
D) To implement incentive mechanisms for storage contribution.
Correct Answer: B

## MCQ 2
Question: What technology is planned to be used for decentralized file storage?
A) Blockchain
B) Centralized servers
C) IPFS
D) Smart contracts
Correct Answer: C


## MCQ 3
Question: What are the vulnerabilities of centralized file-sharing systems mentioned in the text?
A) Slow transfer speeds and limited storage capacity
B) Server downtime and cyberattacks on a central repository
C) Lack of user-friendly interface and complex navigation
D) High cost of maintenance and difficulty in scaling
Correct Answer: B


## MCQ 4
Question: How does the platform plan to manage user permissions and file access?
A) Through centralized server administration
B) Using smart contracts on a blockchain
C) By relying on user self-regulation
D) Through a combination of IPFS and centralized databases
Correct Answer: B
