What is SmartQgen?

+

SmartQgen is a platform that streamlines the creation of quizzes from uploaded documents. It allows users to upload files in formats such as PDF, TXT, and DOCX, and then generate quizzes by specifying parameters like the number of questions and the desired difficulty level.

What are the benefits of using SmartQgen?

+

The benefits of using SmartQgen include its efficiency in automating the quiz generation process, which saves time and effort. It also offers customization options, allowing users to specify the number of questions and their difficulty. Furthermore, SmartQgen provides versatility by supporting multiple file types for uploads and generating different question types, all within a user friendly interface.

What kind of Questions can I prepare?

+

SmartQgen enables the creation of Multiple Choice Questions (MCQs), Fill in the blanks, Numericals and Short Answer questions.

Is SmartQgen easy to use?

+

Yes, SmartQgen is designed with simplicity and ease of use in mind. Our intuitive interface and helpful tutorials will guide you through the process of creating and managing your quizzes.

Is my data safe with SmartQgen?

+

We take data security very seriously. SmartQgen employs industry-standard encryption and security measures to ensure that your data is safe and protected.
